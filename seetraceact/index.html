Abstract

Vision-language-action models (VLAs) are promising general-purpose robot policies, but adapting them to new tasks typically requires costly task-specific teleoperation data. As an alternative, we study one-shot demo-conditioned VLAs, where a robot policy is conditioned on a single demonstration video of an unseen task. We find that existing end-to-end approaches often struggle when successful execution requires precisely localizing small target regions. To address this limitation, we propose SeeTraceAct, a demo-conditioned VLA framework that encourages precise spatial grounding through visibility-aware prediction of future end-effector traces. To enable reproducible evaluation with cross-embodiment demonstrations, we introduce and release RoboCasa-DC, a demo-conditioned extension of RoboCasa that pairs its robot trajectories with newly collected humanoid demonstrations. Experiments on RoboCasa-DC and a real-world benchmark, where a Franka Panda arm is conditioned on human demonstrations, show that SeeTraceAct outperforms baselines, achieving the best success rate across all four RoboCasa-DC settings and improving real-world average success by 9.5 percentage points.

Method

1Video tokens

V-JEPA 2 encodes the demonstration video and a Perceiver Resampler compresses it into a compact set of tokens that join the VLM input.

2Visual latent plan

Learnable query tokens attend to the image, language, and video tokens. Their hidden states condition the action expert.

3Visibility-aware traces

The plan is decoded into future end-effector traces in each camera view. A validity head predicts whether each point is in frame, so off-screen points still carry a signal.

Training only. The trace decoder shapes the plan during training and is discarded at inference, so the deployed policy simply sees and acts.

RoboCasa-DC Benchmark

A demo-conditioned extension of RoboCasa: every Panda-arm trajectory is paired with a GR-1 humanoid demonstration.

24 kitchen tasks
100 Panda–humanoid demo pairs per task
50 evaluation seeds per task, each with its own demo

Two demo sources

  • Same-embodiment Panda-arm video
  • Cross-embodiment GR-1 humanoid video as a human proxy

Two evaluation splits

  • Category-balanced every task type
  • Precision-sensitive tasks with small target regions

Each split trains on 19 tasks and holds out 5 unseen ones.

Public release. RoboCasa-DC is available on Hugging Face.

Demos and traces work together

Dropping either the demonstration video or trace supervision falls well short of the full model. Neither is enough on its own.

Visibility awareness matters

Without the validity head, success drops below even having no trace supervision. Regressing off-screen points hurts.

Simple 2D traces win

Image-space traces of the robot beat 3D traces and demonstrator traces.

Gains grow as the target shrinks

Trained on all 24 RoboCasa-DC tasks, SeeTraceAct's gain over the strongest baseline grows as the target region shrinks.
